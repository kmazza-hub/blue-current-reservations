# Blue Current V34.6.7 — Predictive Service Center

V34.6.7 adds a forward-looking service forecast built on the operational context, digital twin, orchestration, and portfolio intelligence layers.

## Implemented
- 15, 30, and 60 minute operating forecasts
- Projected occupancy, kitchen load, wait time, ticket time, and pressure
- Emerging risk-window detection
- Preemptive intervention recommendations
- Evidence and forecast-confidence reporting
- Human approval workflow for recommended moves
- EventBus integration and AppState persistence

The forecast is advisory and uses the current application state. It does not directly alter live restaurant systems.
