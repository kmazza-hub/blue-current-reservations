# V79.75.0 — Integration Drift, Recovery & Continuity

V79.75 protects the operating picture after a provider has already become trusted.

Blue Current now detects:
- provider feed delay
- provider feed outage
- mapping loss
- degraded provider health
- rejection-rate spikes
- duplicate-rate spikes
- loss of reconciliation confidence

Continuity states:
- STABLE
- WATCH
- DEGRADED
- CRITICAL

Fallback behavior:
- TRUSTED_LIVE
- LIVE_WITH_CAUTION
- DEGRADED_LOCAL_FALLBACK
- LOCAL_FALLBACK

A provider that drifts cannot automatically promote itself back to trusted live. Explicit revalidation is required.

Local restaurant operations remain available when an external provider is degraded or offline.
