## Closeout review

A closeout review requires a prior human STABLE stabilization decision and records:

- objective/outcome summary
- operator feedback
- guest-impact summary
- incident closeout
- support burden
- data/KPI confidence
- unresolved debt
- lessons learned
- expansion prerequisites

High/critical unresolved debt remains visible and prevents the normal expansion-ready path.

## Human expansion decision

Final human decisions:

- EXPAND
- HOLD
- RETIRE

EXPAND normally requires all 10 closeout/readiness gates.

HOLD and RETIRE require a documented reason.

An EXPAND decision does not deploy another restaurant.

A RETIRE decision does not automatically retire the pilot environment.
