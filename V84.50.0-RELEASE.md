# V84.50.0 — Learning Governance & Playbook Authority

Adds explicit governance around organizational operating knowledge.

Playbook authority is role-based across AUTHOR, REVIEWER, APPROVER, and EXECUTIVE
responsibilities. Governance supports separation of duties, evidence-gated approval,
independent review, suspension, supersession, retirement, and a persistent audit
history.

Approval never authorizes operational execution or automatic cross-location rollout.
