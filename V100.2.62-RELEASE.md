# V100.2.62 — Kitchen First Priority / Exception Intelligence

Kitchen now answers `What should I deal with first?` without introducing fabricated telemetry.

- Ready food always rises first.
- 15+ minutes without Ready becomes `Needs check`.
- 23+ minutes without Ready becomes `Recovery needed`.
- Blue Current states only the known fact: no Ready confirmation has been received.
- Existing `Mark ready` and Service `Food delivered` actions remain human-owned.
- Host/Floor, V100.2.60 Kitchen Truth, and V100.2.61 handoff modules are protected.
