# BLUE CURRENT V45.10.0 — GOVERNED INTERVENTION PLANNING

## Release scope

V45.6–V45.10 advances Autonomous Operations from evidence-backed recommendations and simulation into governed intervention planning. This release still stops before live execution.

### V45.6 — Domain Policy Envelopes
Adds explicit policy envelopes for:
- kitchen throughput
- reservation pacing
- staffing
- guest recovery
- revenue opportunity

Each domain declares:
- allowed proposal types
- risk level
- required approval role
- bounded limits
- prohibited live actions

Examples of explicitly prohibited actions include changing live reservations, publishing staff shifts, modifying kitchen tickets, issuing guest compensation, and publishing revenue offers.

### V45.7 — Intervention Proposal Objects
Recommendations and their simulations can now be converted into persistent intervention proposals.

Every proposal retains:
- source cycle
- source recommendation
- source simulation
- operating evidence
- agent ownership
- domain
- risk
- approval role
- policy limits
- prohibited actions

All proposals remain `governed-dry-run`.

### V45.8 — Cross-Domain Conflict Detection
Adds conflict detection before coordinated interventions can progress.

Current conflict classes include:
- capacity versus demand
- kitchen throughput versus demand stimulation
- duplicate same-domain interventions
- high-risk concurrency sequencing

A high-severity unresolved conflict blocks rehearsal from reaching approval review.

### V45.9 — Multi-Domain Intervention Rehearsal
Creates location-scoped intervention sequences using an operational order:

Kitchen stabilization → staffing coverage → reservation pacing → guest recovery → revenue opportunity.

Rehearsal results are either:
- `blocked`
- `ready-for-approval-review`

They never become executable.

### V45.10 — Intervention Planning Readiness
Adds certification across:
- policy envelopes
- intervention proposal persistence
- simulation linkage
- risk and approval ownership
- conflict detection
- multi-domain rehearsal
- approval hard-stop
- live-state isolation

- intervention readiness certification

## Safety boundary
V45.10.0 introduces no live action executor.

Policy, proposal, conflict, and rehearsal objects carry:
- `liveExecutionAllowed: false`
- `liveStateChanged: false`

A conflict-free rehearsal stops at `ready-for-approval-review`.

## Operation V position
The next logical V45 milestone is governed approval and intervention authorization architecture: policy-aware approval packets, expiry/revalidation, approval scope, revocation, and executable-command drafting. Even there, command drafting should remain distinct from live execution until a separately certified execution boundary is deliberately introduced.
