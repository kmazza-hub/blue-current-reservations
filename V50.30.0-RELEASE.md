## Closure hardening discovered during certification

The final clean-baseline regression exposed a nondeterministic ordering edge case in V50.25 corrective-action evidence: verification and completion could be recorded within the same millisecond, allowing `RISK_REDUCTION_VERIFIED` to sort ahead of the newer `COMPLETION_ACCEPTED` state.

V50.30 hardens evidence ordering with deterministic precedence:

1. created timestamp
2. completion status precedence
3. record ID tie-break

The full V50 suite was rerun after this fix on a clean exact V50.25 baseline.
