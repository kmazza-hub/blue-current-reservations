# Blue Current V100.3.29 — Labor and Inventory API Location Safety

This wave protects the labor and inventory intelligence boundary before pilot use.

- Inventory and workforce snapshots require an explicit authorized restaurant.
- Inventory recommendations, purchase orders, policies, workforce recommendations, and labor plans reject missing or unauthorized restaurant identity.
- Inventory reorder decisions verify that the underlying item belongs to the same organization and restaurant before creating an action or draft order.
- Existing browser clients already submit the active restaurant, preserving normal operator behavior.
- No database or restaurant records are included.
