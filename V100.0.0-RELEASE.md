# V100.0.0 — Blue Current Commercial V1 Certification

V100 establishes the Commercial V1 certification baseline. It consolidates the complete certification
chain from product freeze through technical/operator readiness, release-candidate lock, end-to-end
validation, operations/support readiness, and explicit human GO authorization.

Commercial V1 certification preserves candidate identity and rollback reference and requires an
explicit human certification record. Certification itself performs no deployment and no production
mutation. The V84–V100 build/certification sequence is now closed at the Commercial V1 baseline.
