# Blue Current V35.3.0 — Portfolio Performance Hub

## Business value

Turns cross-location operations into one measurable portfolio view focused on profit, quality, and control. Regional leaders can rank locations by Restaurant Performance Index, identify modeled revenue opportunity, and approve the highest-value interventions.

## Implementation

- Portfolio-level RPI and performance band
- Location-level RPI ranking
- Revenue available, captured, realized, and remaining opportunity
- Cross-location performance drivers
- Highest-value regional actions
- Location drill-down into the Operational Digital Twin
- Outcome Intelligence handoff for approved actions
- AppState persistence and EventBus integration

## Guardrails

All financial values are modeled operational estimates. Portfolio actions require explicit human approval and do not automatically modify connected systems.
