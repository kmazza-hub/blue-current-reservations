# V100.2.80 — Inventory Truth Foundation

## Objective
Establish a restrained primary Inventory operating surface that answers what is recorded on hand, what is below configured par, and whether source data is available before predictive or optimization intelligence.

## Scope
- Adds `client/js/inventory-truth-v100.2.80.js`.
- Registers Inventory as a deferred runtime group.
- Preserves existing Inventory intelligence underneath the truth-first surface.
- Adds no recurring polling, `setInterval`, `setTimeout`, or `MutationObserver`.

Truth before intelligence.
