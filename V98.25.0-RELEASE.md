# V98.25.0 — Commercial Product Freeze & Final Hardening Baseline

V98.25 establishes the commercial scope freeze. After the freeze, changes are limited to explicitly
permitted hardening classes such as severe defects, security, reliability, data integrity,
accessibility, regressions, and certification blockers.

Exceptions require evidence, ownership, and risk classification. High/critical open exceptions block
the freeze gate. Scope expansion, exception approval, and product mutation remain human controlled.

Next gate: Final Regression, Security & Data Integrity Certification.
