# V97.50.0 — Pilot Evidence Quality & Outcome Measurement

V97.50 converts raw field observations into a quality-controlled measurement layer. It measures
evidence completeness, service-night breadth, core-category coverage, verified observations, and
numeric metric series with baseline/current/delta/average values.

Metric movement is explicitly treated as evidence rather than proof of causation. Commercial claims,
product changes, and production mutations remain human decisions.

The gate intentionally remains EVIDENCE_QUALITY_ACCUMULATING until genuine field evidence exists.
