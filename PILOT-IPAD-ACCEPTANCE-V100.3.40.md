# Blue Current iPad Pilot Acceptance

Use one intended pilot iPad in landscape orientation on the restaurant network. Record any failure before advancing; do not improvise around a failed step.

## Install and launch

1. Open the stable HTTPS Blue Current address in Safari.
2. Use Share → Add to Home Screen and confirm the Blue Current icon and name.
3. Launch from the Home Screen and confirm there is no clipped content around the status bar or Home indicator.
4. Sign in and confirm the selected Chefs International location is correct.

## Frontline loop

1. Open Reservations and add a named two-person reservation.
2. Mark the reservation arrived and confirm it leaves Arrivals appropriately.
3. Add a walk-in to Waitlist and confirm the quoted wait is readable.
4. Seat one party and confirm the card clears and the chosen table immediately shows the correct guest.
5. Open full-screen Floor, switch Main Dining Room, Waterfront, and Private Dining, and operate a table in each section.
6. Open Service, Kitchen, and Staff one at a time; confirm each opens, scrolls, accepts its intended action, returns Back, and reopens without freezing.

## Device interruption

1. Background Blue Current for two minutes, return, and confirm the active workspace remains usable.
2. Rotate portrait and back to landscape; confirm controls remain visible and centered.
3. Focus a text field, open the keyboard, and confirm Save and Cancel remain reachable.
4. Briefly disable Wi-Fi, attempt no destructive workaround, restore Wi-Fi, and confirm the connection indicator and current server truth recover.
5. Lock and unlock the iPad; confirm the session either resumes safely or requests a normal sign-in.
