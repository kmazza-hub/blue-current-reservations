# Blue Current V35.13.0 — Margin Intelligence & Profit Control

## Business value
Blue Current now prioritizes operating decisions by modeled contribution-margin impact rather than revenue alone. The release connects captured revenue with food cost, labor, discounts, waste, capacity, and measured outcomes.

## Implemented
- Margin-health score and confidence
- Modeled contribution margin
- Food, labor, discount, and waste cost assumptions
- Profit-leakage ranking
- Projected profit opportunity
- Realized-profit estimate from measured outcomes
- Human-governed profit actions
- Executive Workflow handoff
- AppState persistence and EventBus integration

## Guardrails
All financial outputs are operational decision-support estimates. They are not audited accounting records and must be reconciled with POS, payroll, inventory, and general-ledger data before financial reporting.
