# V82.75.0 — Expansion Stabilization & Early-Life Support Control

Creates the controlled early-life support window after an expansion location is
activated in production.

Stabilization requires:
- launch and support ownership
- confirmed support coverage
- rollback drill readiness
- daily review
- stable trusted provider continuity
- incident thresholds
- a minimum stabilization period

Launch incidents are tracked independently and can block graduation.

A location becomes READY_TO_GRADUATE only after the stabilization window and
safety gates pass. Graduation requires an explicit human decision.

Graduation does not authorize autonomous production changes or broader provider
authority.
