# Blue Current V38.5.7 — Shift Intelligence Loop Hotfix

Fixes a synchronous AppState/EventBus recursion triggered when the Operations pack activates Shift Intelligence.

## Root cause

`ShiftIntelligenceEngine` listened to `state:updated`. Its calculation then called `AppState.update()`, which synchronously emitted another `state:updated` event. The engine immediately re-entered itself until Edge reported that the page was not responding.

## Resolution

- Added a strict re-entry guard around Shift Intelligence calculation and publication.
- Preserved live recalculation from operating-state updates.
- Bumped the Shift Intelligence asset cache version to 38.5.7.
