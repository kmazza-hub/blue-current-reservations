Common review questions

Start the diligence conversation with clear boundaries.

Does Blue Current replace our systems of record?

No. Blue Current is designed as a connected operating layer. Pilot integrations can be limited to the specific signals required for the approved workflow.

Can high-impact AI actions require human approval?

Yes. Approval requirements, operating modes, confidence thresholds, role permissions, and rollback paths can be defined around each workflow.

How are actions and decisions reviewed?

The platform is designed to preserve source context, policy evaluation, ownership, approval, action, and observed outcome as connected operating evidence.

What happens when connectivity or a service degrades?

Blue Current includes offline continuity, version reconciliation, observability, incident command, and recovery workflows as part of its reliability foundation.
