# WEB-032 — Brand Authority & Pilot Proof

Adds the Blue Current Standard, system-fit positioning, and a measurable pilot-proof framework without invented logos, testimonials, or performance claims.

Principles: Hospitality before automation; Service speed before software complexity; Evidence before autonomy; Connect before replace.

Pilot evidence covers guest, service, operator, and leadership outcomes. Expansion remains a human evidence-based decision: Expand / Hold / Extend / Retire.
