# Blue Current V100.3.35 — Frontline Intelligence Tenant Truth

This closing wave removes synthetic workforce records and independently protects workforce and inventory calculations at the service layer.

## Workforce truth

- Removed the generated twelve-person staffing fallback.
- Workforce totals now contain only stored staff records for the selected organization and restaurant.
- Missing skill, reliability, shift-time, section, and hourly-rate values are no longer fabricated.
- Zero recorded demand remains zero instead of being forced to one.
- Labor actions and open shift offers now require matching organization and restaurant identity.
- Missing or foreign restaurant identities are rejected before workforce decisions or labor-plan changes.

## Inventory truth

- Inventory snapshots now verify the restaurant belongs to the organization.
- Kitchen demand signals exclude tickets belonging to another organization.
- Purchase orders no longer silently default to Marina when restaurant identity is missing.

No database files are included.
