# V91.25.0 — Command Information Hierarchy & Role Focus

V91.25 gives the shared Blue Current Command surface four presentation profiles: Host,
Manager, Operator, and Executive.

Role focus changes information hierarchy, not authorization. Hosts prioritize guest flow
and do not see pilot controls or evidence. Managers prioritize live service and controlled
actions. Operators prioritize pilot integrity, incidents, and evidence. Executives see
readiness, outcomes, and exceptions without service-level pilot controls.

The role preference is stored locally in the browser. All views remain grounded in the
same backend truth and all server-side permissions/guardrails remain unchanged.
