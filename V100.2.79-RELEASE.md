# V100.2.79 — Time Clock Rush-Condition Certification

## Objective
Certify the Time Clock truth lifecycle under rapid multi-employee restaurant conditions after V100.2.76 truth foundation, V100.2.77 record integrity, and V100.2.78 identity integrity.

## Certified behavior
- rapid multi-employee clock-ins remain isolated;
- duplicate active clock-ins are rejected;
- simultaneous breaks remain attached to the correct timecards;
- ending/closing one employee state does not contaminate another;
- manager corrections preserve completed vs. needs-review truth;
- needs-review records do not masquerade as active workers;
- location and organization boundaries remain isolated;
- Time Clock record identities remain unique and correctly owned;
- realtime and audit transitions remain present;
- Staffing consumes only Time Clock active truth and excludes active breaks;
- Staffing still requires published Scheduling truth.

## Architecture
Certification only. No runtime application files are changed.
