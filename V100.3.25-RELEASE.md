# V100.3.25 — Executive Navigation Location Integrity

Prevents executive review and focus controls from silently navigating to Marina when portfolio identity is incomplete.

## Certified improvements

- Excludes locationless district cards from Executive Analytics ranking.
- Excludes locationless district cards from Executive Scorecards.
- Excludes locationless district cards from Executive Daily Brief priority selection.
- Clears and disables review controls when no verified restaurant is available.
- Rejects empty review navigation instead of falling back to another restaurant.

## Preserved

- Executive analytics, scoring, prioritization, and daily briefing remain portfolio-wide.
- Valid district cards continue to navigate to their own restaurant.
- Existing calculations, score weights, charts, printing, and observation behavior remain unchanged.
- No server route, database record, authorization list, or restaurant state is modified.
