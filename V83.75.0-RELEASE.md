# V83.75.0 — Executive Decision Outcome Intelligence

Blue Current can now analyze the governed executive decision ledger to surface:

- intervention effectiveness by decision type
- recurring operational issues
- repeated weak outcomes across multiple locations
- leadership accountability and outcome history
- overdue executive follow-up
- systemic patterns that deserve executive review

This layer is descriptive decision intelligence. It does not select decisions,
judge personnel, execute operational changes, expand rollout automatically, or
change production authority.
