# V96.50.0 — Commercial Hardening: Defect, Friction & Regression Control

V96.50 creates the commercial-hardening issue-control layer. Pilot defects, operator friction, and
regressions now share an explicit severity model, release-blocking rules, evidence requirements, and
human verification lifecycle. Critical and high issues block release; resolved issues are not treated
as closed until verified. This preserves the V96 baseline while turning real-world pilot observations
into controlled commercial-release work.
