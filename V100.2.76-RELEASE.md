# V100.2.76 — Time & Attendance Truth Foundation

## Objective
Make the primary Time Clock experience answer a factual operating question first: **Who is actually working right now?**

## Changes
- Adds a truth-first Time Clock surface sourced from the existing Time Clock API.
- Shows recorded active clock-ins, breaks, today's timecards, and manager correction history.
- Preserves existing clock-in, clock-out, break-start, break-end, and correction endpoints.
- Excludes labor-cost projections, overtime prediction, and inferred attendance causes from the primary view.
- Loads through the existing deferred Staff runtime group and activates on Time Clock navigation/visibility.
- Adds no `setInterval`, `setTimeout`, or `MutationObserver`.
- Does not modify `server/services/timeClockService.js`, `client/js/modules/timeClock.js`, Staffing truth, Scheduling truth, Host/Floor, Service, Kitchen, or Manager Operations.
