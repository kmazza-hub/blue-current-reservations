# V100.3.46 — Physical Walkthrough Console

This release closes the gap between reaching Blue Current on a physical iPad and recording trustworthy pilot evidence.

- Adds an opt-in, full-screen physical walkthrough at `?pilotAcceptance=1`.
- Guides all ten frontline workflow steps and records device, environment, outcome, note, and timestamp evidence.
- Separates LAN evidence from hosted-pilot evidence and makes friction and blockers explicit.
- Allows final human signoff only after every server-side acceptance gate passes.
- Leaves normal Host, Floor, Service, Kitchen, and Staff screens unchanged.

No restaurant database payload, automatic deployment, or simulated physical evidence is included.
