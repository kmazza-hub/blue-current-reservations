# BLUE CURRENT V45.25.0 — CONTROLLED EXECUTION CERTIFICATION

## Release scope

V45.21–V45.25 builds the certification layer around the existing shadow execution boundary. It proves policy, authorization, observation, compensation, circuit breaker, and promotion controls while keeping live restaurant mutation disabled.

### V45.21 — Immutable Controlled-Execution Policy
Adds a hashed immutable policy declaring:
- certification-only mode
- live execution disabled
- dual authorization required
- minimum 15-minute observation window
- zero-percent maximum live canary
- circuit-breaker thresholds
- promotion criteria
- adapter certification status

### V45.22 — Dual Authorization
Execution certification now requires two distinct approvers.

Approvers must be General Manager or higher.

A duplicate approver cannot satisfy both authorization slots.

### V45.23 — Observation Window + Circuit Breaker
Certification supports:
- starting an observation window
- completing the observation window
- explicit circuit-breaker trip
- reset-for-review after a breaker drill

Circuit-breaker trip immediately makes the certification ineligible for promotion.

### V45.24 — Compensation Validation
Allowlisted adapters can run compensation-contract drills.

A successful compensation drill is required by promotion criteria.

The drill is simulation-only and cannot touch restaurant state.

### V45.25 — Promotion Criteria + Controlled Execution Readiness
Certification evaluates:
- dual authorization
- completed observation window
- successful compensation drill
- minimum shadow-run evidence
- successful shadow preflight
- idempotency evidence
- emergency-stop engagement
- absence of live handlers
- untripped circuit breaker

A successful certification may reach:

`certified-shadow-boundary`

This means the safety architecture is ready for future controlled-release design. It does NOT enable live execution.

## Safety boundary
Live mutation remains impossible through the V45 controlled-execution path.

Current certification policy requires:
- `liveExecutionEnabled: false`
- zero live canary traffic
- emergency stop engaged
- all production/live handlers absent
