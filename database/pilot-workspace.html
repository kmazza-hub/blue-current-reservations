          Four-week cadence

          Fast enough to learn. Controlled enough to trust.

          Week 1

          Assess

          Confirm scope, baseline, owners, systems, controls, and success measures.

          Week 2

          Configure

          Connect minimum data, set permissions, and test escalation paths.

          Week 3

          Operate

          Run the workflow with daily observation and human review.

          Week 4

          Decide

          Compare results with baseline and recommend expansion, iteration, or stop.

          Executive deliverables

          A decision package—not a vague demo recap.

          What changed, why it mattered, and whether the outcome supports expansion.

          02

          Operational findings

          Workflow friction, interventions, risks, and ownership gaps.

          03

          ROI review

          Observed results compared with the agreed baseline.

          04

          Deployment recommendation

          Expand, iterate, or stop—with explicit rationale.

          05

          Expansion roadmap

          Next locations, workflows, integrations, and decision gates.
