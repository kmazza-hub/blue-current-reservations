# Blue Current V34.6.8 — Autonomous Policy Center

V34.6.8 introduces governed autonomous operations. Predictive signals can now be evaluated against explicit operating policies, producing approval-gated actions with evidence, ownership, confidence, and decision history.

## Included
- Policy evaluation engine
- Configurable enable/disable controls
- Predictive-service and portfolio triggers
- Approval-gated execution
- Evidence and confidence display
- AppState persistence
- EventBus integration
- Orchestration handoff after approval

No action executes without explicit manager approval.
