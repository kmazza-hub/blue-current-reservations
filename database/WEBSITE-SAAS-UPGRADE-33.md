# WEB-033 — Profitability, Calm Operations & Final Homepage Hierarchy

WEB-033 sharpens Blue Current's public value proposition around restaurant economics and operating control without guaranteeing financial outcomes.

## Economic operating story
- Capture demand
- Use capacity better
- Reduce operating drag
- Protect the next visit

## Operating model
Detect -> Explain -> Act -> Measure

The website now explicitly connects Blue Current's operating intelligence to the moments where restaurants can lose time, capacity, guest demand, labor efficiency, and future visits.

A financial disclaimer makes clear that Blue Current provides operating signals and decision support; outcomes depend on restaurant execution and market conditions.

The final brand thesis is:
"Give hospitality teams more control without giving them more chaos."
