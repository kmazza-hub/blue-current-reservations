# V83.50.0 — Portfolio Decision Ledger & Executive Accountability

Adds an executive decision ledger linked to portfolio exceptions.

Each decision records:
- exception and location
- decision type
- rationale
- expected outcome
- accountable owner
- required follow-up time
- decision maker

Follow-up reviews record the observed outcome and rate the decision as EFFECTIVE,
PARTIAL, or INEFFECTIVE.

Decision records are accountability and governance artifacts. They do not execute
operational actions, change location authority, or authorize autonomous production
changes.
