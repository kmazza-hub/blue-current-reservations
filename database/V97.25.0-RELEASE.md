# V97.25.0 — Live Pilot Execution & Field Evidence Foundation

V97.25 turns actual restaurant service into structured product evidence. Observations are tied to a
service night and location and classified across operator friction, guest impact, workflow performance,
system intervention, incident, recovery, and operational outcome. Evidence may include measurable
metrics, but it cannot automatically mutate the product or authorize a release.

The gate intentionally remains evidence-accumulating until real field observations exist.
