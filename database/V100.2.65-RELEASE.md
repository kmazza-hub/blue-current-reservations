# V100.2.65 — Published Schedule → Live Role Coverage

Adds trustworthy staffing coverage on top of V100.2.64.

- Expected staffing = published shifts active at the current time.
- Actual staffing = clocked-in Time Clock employees not on break.
- Role gaps are simple expected-minus-actual arithmetic.
- Draft/unpublished schedules are explicitly not certified.
- No reservation-demand formula, forecast, or synthetic staffing requirement is used.
- Existing Scheduling, Time Clock, Staff Truth, Kitchen, and Host/Floor runtime files remain protected.
