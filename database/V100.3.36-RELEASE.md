# Blue Current V100.3.36 — Labor Record Assignment Integrity

This closing wave protects schedule, workforce, time-clock, and employee-portal records from cross-restaurant assignment at the service layer.

## Scheduling

- Schedule snapshots, publishing, and week copying now independently verify restaurant ownership.
- Shift creation and reassignment reject employees from another organization or restaurant.
- Valid same-restaurant shift assignment and Smart Fill behavior remain intact.

## Workforce and Time Clock

- Workforce snapshots, employee creation, and shift-template creation verify restaurant ownership.
- Time Clock snapshots no longer silently default to Marina.
- Clock-in requires the employee’s exact restaurant and stores that authoritative identity.
- Clock-out and break actions can no longer consume active records from another restaurant.

## Employee Portal

- Portal sessions are rejected if employee organization or restaurant identity has drifted.
- Swap requests verify both the assigned shift and optional target employee belong to the same restaurant.

No UI navigation or database payload is changed.
