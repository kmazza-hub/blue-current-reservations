Integration questions

Clear expectations before technical discovery.

Does Blue Current require us to replace our POS or reservation platform?

No. Blue Current is designed to coordinate across existing platforms while those systems retain their established responsibilities.

Are every listed vendor and connector production-ready today?

No unsupported connector claim is made on this page. Specific APIs, permissions, environments, and deployment readiness are confirmed during technical discovery.

How much data is required for a pilot?

The pilot is scoped to the minimum signals needed for one measurable workflow. Broader access is not required to establish value.

How are failures and synchronization handled?

The architecture includes observability, retry and reconciliation patterns, incident ownership, audit history, and explicit recovery procedures.
