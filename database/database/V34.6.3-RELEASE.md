# Blue Current V34.6.3 — Operational Digital Twin

V34.6.3 introduces a live, explainable model of the current restaurant operation. It combines table state, dining-room zones, kitchen stations, workforce utilization, active AI workflows, and a 30-minute projection into one operating picture.

## Implemented
- Event-driven Operational Digital Twin Engine
- Live table entities with status, party size, elapsed time, turn prediction, zone, and risk
- Zone occupancy and at-risk counts
- Kitchen station load and ticket-time projections
- Workforce role utilization
- Composite health score
- 30-minute arrivals, turns, occupancy, and kitchen forecast
- AppState persistence and EventBus publication
- Interactive Digital Twin Center inside the AI Brain

All forecasts are operational estimates derived from current demo state and are not guarantees.
