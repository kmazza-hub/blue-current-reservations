# Blue Current V35.11.0

## Performance Governance & Operating Cadence

This release converts expansion evidence into a repeatable enterprise management rhythm. It adds daily, weekly, and monthly review cadences, accountable owners, measurable operating commitments, governance health scoring, blockers, watch conditions, and exportable executive-review evidence.

### Business value
- Keeps profit, quality, adoption, and operating control visible in one management cadence.
- Assigns clear ownership to reviews and commitments.
- Prevents expansion evidence from becoming a passive dashboard.
- Gives executive leadership a repeatable operating review pack.

### Safeguards
- Blocking post-launch issues remain visible.
- Expansion blockers force governance attention.
- Unassigned high-priority commitments prevent a dependable governance state.
- Financial values remain operational estimates and measured evidence, not audited accounting records.
