# Blue Current V35.10.0 — Expansion Control, Benchmarking & Continuous Improvement

## Business value
Turns post-launch evidence into a governed expansion decision. Leadership can compare locations, identify repeatable operating wins, preserve rollout quality, and scale only when adoption, performance, measured value, and model confidence support it.

## Implemented
- Cross-location performance and adoption benchmarks
- Scaling-ready location count
- Repeatable operating playbooks
- Expansion readiness score and gate
- Expansion blockers and watch conditions
- Location-wave planning, ownership, status, and approval
- Exportable expansion evidence manifest
- AppState and EventBus integration
- High-contrast responsive Expansion Control Center

## Governance
Expansion remains a human decision. Blocking post-launch issues force a hold state. Financial values remain operating estimates and measured evidence, not audited accounting records.
