# Blue Current V34.7.0 — Executive Workflow Engine

V34.7.0 introduces governed, multi-step operational workflows that connect AI recommendations and approved autonomous policies to accountable execution.

## Implemented

- Workflow templates for kitchen pacing, service recovery, and portfolio overflow
- Step dependencies and ordered progression
- Human approval checkpoints
- Pause and resume controls
- SLA deadlines and overdue visibility
- Named workflow ownership
- Supporting evidence and source context
- Persistent workflow state through AppState
- Full workflow audit trail
- Completion history and outcome evidence
- EventBus integration with Autonomous Policy and Predictive Service decisions
- Executive Workflow Center inside the authenticated application

No workflow bypasses its configured approval checkpoint. V34.7.0 remains human-governed.
