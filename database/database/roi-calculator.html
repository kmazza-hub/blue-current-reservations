Methodology

A useful business case separates opportunity from proof.

The calculator deliberately keeps assumptions visible. A real pilot replaces each estimate with verified source data and measures the observed result.

01

Frame the leak

Choose one economic problem with an identifiable owner, source system, and operational consequence.

02

Validate the baseline

Use call, reservation, labor, service, or reporting data to replace illustrative assumptions.

03

Deploy one workflow

Keep the pilot bounded, governed, and measurable instead of attempting a broad transformation.

04

Review the evidence

Compare the observed outcome with the baseline and make an explicit expand, revise, or stop decision.
