# Blue Current V34.6.5 — Portfolio Intelligence Center

V34.6.5 extends the Operational Digital Twin from one restaurant into a cross-location operating picture.

## Implemented
- Portfolio health aggregation across six locations
- Cross-location occupancy, guest, revenue, kitchen, labor, and pressure modeling
- Critical and watch exception detection
- Regional response windows and evidence summaries
- Cross-location demand-rebalancing recommendations
- Human approval for portfolio actions
- Location drill-down into the existing Operational Digital Twin
- AppState persistence and EventBus updates

## Safety
The release is advisory. Portfolio actions require an explicit operator approval and do not change production systems automatically.
