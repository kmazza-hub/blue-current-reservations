# Blue Current V41.2.0 — Hospitality Ontology & Decision Intelligence

## V41.0 — Hospitality Ontology
Defines a governed operating language for guests, reservations, tables, kitchen tickets, employees, decisions, and locations.

## V41.1 — Decision Object Registry
Turns recommendations and interventions into durable business objects with evidence, ownership, approvals, ontology links, expected outcomes, and lifecycle history.

## V41.2 — Causal Decision Trace
Connects evidence to a decision and its expected effects so AI guidance is explainable, reviewable, and exportable.

## Safety
This release does not execute restaurant changes automatically. Decision approval remains explicit and human-governed.
