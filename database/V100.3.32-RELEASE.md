# Blue Current V100.3.32 — Live Service Record Authorization

This wave protects the Service, Kitchen, and Staff focused workspaces at their authenticated mutation boundary.

- Each workspace requires explicit authorized restaurant identity for reads and new records.
- Service-flow updates and delivery authorize the stored flow before mutation.
- Service creation verifies its table and assigned staff belong to the same restaurant and organization.
- Kitchen ticket and item updates authorize the stored ticket and require write permission.
- Staff edits, section assignments, and table reassignments verify organization, restaurant match, and operator access.
- No database or restaurant records are included.
