# V84.0.0 — Portfolio Learning & Operating Playbook Intelligence

Converts reviewed executive decision outcomes into governed organizational learning.

Blue Current can now identify evidence-backed learning patterns and surface strong
patterns as operating playbook candidates. Humans author the guidance, approve it,
and can retire it.

A playbook is reference guidance only. Historical success never grants Blue Current
authority to execute an operational action or roll a practice across locations.
Local context and human authority remain required.
