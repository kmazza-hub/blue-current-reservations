V89 adds the human operating gate after service simulation. Blue Current cannot approve
itself for pilot progression.

handling, service fit, and trust. Every dimension requires observation, averages must
meet the acceptance threshold, and unresolved blockers prevent acceptance.

changes remain disabled.
