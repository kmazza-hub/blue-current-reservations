# Blue Current V35.2.0 — Executive Briefing

## Business value

Blue Current now converts the Restaurant Performance Index, measured outcomes, forecast risks, portfolio exceptions, approvals, and workflows into one leadership briefing. The objective is to let an operator understand performance, lost opportunity, urgent decisions, and verified improvement in under 30 seconds.

## Implementation

- Executive Briefing Engine
- Executive Briefing Center
- RPI, opportunity, realized revenue, approvals, and confidence synthesis
- Leadership questions answered in plain operating language
- Ranked priorities with projected revenue and RPI effect
- Risks, exceptions, and measured wins
- Governed action handoff into Outcome Intelligence
- Copy/download-ready text briefing
- AppState and EventBus integration

## Accuracy

Financial values are modeled operational estimates. They are not accounting records or guaranteed results. Measured revenue is sourced from the existing Outcome Intelligence model.
