# WEB-029 — Hospitality Storyfront

Blue Current's public homepage now tells the complete Hospitality OS story rather than positioning the product primarily around reservations or isolated modules.

## Story architecture

**Hero:** Hospitality moves fast. Blue Current moves with it.

**The Guest Current**
- AI Concierge
- Reservations
- Guest Intelligence
- Host Stand & Floor

**The Service Current**
- Live Command
- Workforce
- Kitchen Command
- Service Coordination

**The Intelligence Current**
- Restaurant AI Brain
- Predictive Operations
- Executive Command
- Governed Automation

**Enterprise foundations**
Authentication/RBAC, audit, offline recovery, observability, connector sync, data contracts, reconciliation, incident command, runbooks/SLOs, release certification, pilot governance, multi-location control.

The public navigation is intentionally reduced to the buyer story: Why Blue Current, Guest, Operations, Intelligence, Enterprise, Trust, and the demo CTA.
