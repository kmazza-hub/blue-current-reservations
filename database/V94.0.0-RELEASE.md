# V94.0.0 — Pilot Performance, Capacity & Resilience Readiness

V94.0 certifies Blue Current's existing peak-service stress and workflow-resilience systems as a
formal live-pilot gate. Ten service-pressure and failure scenarios are covered, including reservation
bursts, high occupancy, kitchen pressure, API/connector failures, reconnect/retry, and partial
degradation recovery. Human observations verify handoff latency, operator workload, kitchen/floor
congestion, recovery time, service completion, and unresolved high/critical findings.

No automatic restaurant mutation or autonomous production change is introduced.
