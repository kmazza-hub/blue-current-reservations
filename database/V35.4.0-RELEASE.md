# Blue Current V35.4.0 — Learning, Calibration & Pilot Hardening

## Business value

V35.4.0 closes the intelligence loop. Blue Current now uses measured outcomes to calibrate recommendation confidence, identify weak evidence domains, and report whether the platform is ready for a customer pilot.

## Implemented

- Performance Learning Engine
- Forecast calibration score
- Success-rate and realization tracking
- Domain-level learning for demand, kitchen, guest, and labor decisions
- Recommendation-confidence adjustment from measured evidence
- Pilot-readiness scorecard and safeguards
- Calibration recommendations
- AppState and EventBus integration
- Restaurant Performance Engine confidence integration
- High-contrast responsive Learning & Pilot Hardening Center

## Governance

Confidence adjustments are bounded. Limited evidence cannot create a positive confidence increase, and all restaurant actions remain subject to their existing human approval controls.
