# Blue Current V35.9.0

## Post-Launch Command, Adoption & Value Realization

V35.9.0 extends the governed deployment flow into the first weeks after go-live. It gives operators and executives one place to monitor launch health, user adoption, unresolved rollout issues, realized operating value, and expansion readiness.

### Business value

- Keeps launch problems visible and assigned.
- Measures whether operators are actually using Blue Current.
- Connects realized revenue and RPI recovery to the rollout.
- Shows which locations are ready to stabilize or scale.
- Creates an exportable evidence package for executive reviews.

### New modules

- `client/js/modules/postLaunchValueEngine.js`
- `client/js/modules/postLaunchValueCenter.js`

### Safeguards

- The module does not bypass human approvals.
- Revenue values remain modeled or measured operating evidence, not accounting records.
- Blocking post-launch issues prevent a scaling-ready state.
- Weak adoption and forecast evidence remain visible as watch conditions.
