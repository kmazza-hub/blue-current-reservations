# WEB-030 — Hospitality OS Story Expansion

WEB-030 preserves Blue Current's current editorial hospitality identity and expands the public story beyond reservation calls.

The cream/navy palette, Playfair editorial typography, interactive dinner-service demonstration, guest journey, host stand, analytics, and executive experiences remain intact.

New public narrative:
- Guest experience remains the emotional entry point.
- Live Command explains the in-service operating picture.
- Workforce explains coverage, callout risk, redeployment, and scheduling.
- Kitchen Command explains station pressure, ticket flow, expo, pacing, and recommended moves.
- Service Coordination explains active tables, food-ready queues, server load, and recovery.
- Restaurant AI Brain explains recommendation, evidence, confidence, human control, and outcome measurement.
- Enterprise foundations surface RBAC, audit, offline recovery, observability, connectors, data contracts, reconciliation, incident command, runbooks/SLOs, release certification, pilot governance, and multi-location control.

Public navigation keeps the hospitality presentation and now exposes Operations and AI Brain without turning the homepage into an engineering dashboard.
