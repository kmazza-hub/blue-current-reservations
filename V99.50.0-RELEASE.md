# V99.50.0 — Commercial Operations & Support Readiness

V99.50 certifies the operating model surrounding the locked and end-to-end validated commercial
release candidate. Named support, escalation, and deployment owners are required alongside support,
incident, and recovery runbooks and defined business-hours/on-call coverage.

Critical active incidents and incomplete recovery reviews block readiness. Operations/support
readiness is explicitly human accepted and does not authorize commercial release.
