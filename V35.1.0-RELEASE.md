# Blue Current V35.1.0 — Outcome Intelligence

## Business value
Blue Current now measures whether approved operational actions improved restaurant performance. It connects a decision to realized revenue, RPI movement, and forecast accuracy.

## Implemented
- Active outcome measurement windows
- Baseline and projected impact capture
- Realized revenue and RPI measurement
- Successful, partial, and underperforming classifications
- Forecast accuracy and success-rate calculation
- AppState persistence and EventBus integration
- Compatibility events for the existing learning/calibration layer
- Outcome Intelligence Center inside the authenticated application

All financial values remain operational estimates until connected to verified accounting and POS data.
