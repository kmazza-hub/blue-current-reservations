# V94.50.0 — Pilot Training, Runbook & Operator Enablement Readiness

V94.50 consolidates the existing operator-experience certification, pilot deployment procedures,
and current pilot operator acceptance into a single enablement gate. A pilot location cannot be
considered enablement-ready without live-service usability, acceptable training burden, shift
handoff, readable/accessibile surfaces, operator and manager acceptance, plus access, backup/restore,
startup/restart, support/escalation, rollback, and deployment-evidence procedures.

Training never grants permissions or executes restaurant actions. Human acceptance remains required.
